# Verified rule sources (2026-11-17 Asia/Shanghai)

## M-League current official rules

Source: <https://m-league.jp/about/>; HTML: `mleague-direct.html`; extracted text: `mleague-about.txt`.

- Chapter 1, art.1: 「競技は1卓4人で行う、東南二風の半荘を以って1回戦とする。」
- Chapter 1, art.2: 「5萬・5筒・5索の各1枚を赤牌とする。」
- Chapter 2, art.6: regular dora, kan-dora, ura and kan-ura are all specified.
- Chapter 3, art.11: 「いかなる場合でも、局の途中で流局はしない。」; tenpai pool 3000.
- Chapter 3, art.12: 「連荘は親のアガリと聴牌宣言を行った時とする。親がノーテンの場合は、下家に親が移動する。」
- Chapter 3, art.13: honba after dealer repeat or draw; 300 per honba; reset after nondealer win.
- Chapter 4, art.5: 「リーチ後の暗槓は面子構成の変わらない暗槓なら可能。(役の増減は問わない)」
- Chapter 4, art.5: kan-dora opens as soon as the kan is accepted, never for a robbed kan.
- Chapter 4, art.5: 「いかなる場合でも、暗槓の搶槓は成立しない。」 (also excludes kokushi robbery).
- Chapter 4, art.8: 「フリテンリーチやツモ番のないリーチもかける事ができる。(海底牌を摸した者はリーチを掛ける事はできない。)」
- Chapter 5, art.1: one winner only, discarder-left / opposite / right order (atamahane).
- Chapter 5, art.4: 「異なる役満が複合した場合も認められる。」
- Chapter 6, art.1-2: start 25000, return 30000. Rank awards +50000/+10000/-10000/-30000 include oka. 1000 points = 1 match point.
- Chapter 6, art.2: tied ranks split awards; three-way fractional remainder favors the seat closer to initial dealer.
- Chapter 6, art.2: end-of-match deposits awarded to first; tied first split them. Three-way 1000 => 400/300/300; 2000 => 800/600/600, initial-dealer-nearest gets the larger portion.
- Chapter 6, art.3: 「連風牌の対子も2符とする」.
- Chapter 6, art.4/6: 4 han 30 fu and 3 han 60 fu round up to mangan.
- Chapter 6, art.6: 「役満以外の役が複合したアガリ点は、三倍満までを上限とする。」 No counted yakuman.
- Chapter 8: pao applies to daisangen, daisuushii **and suukantsu**, with concealed kans counted among prior public declarations. Tsumo full responsibility; ron split responsibility. Honba charged to responsible seat. For combined yakuman, only the completed responsible yakuman is paid by responsible seat and remaining yakuman are paid normally.
- Chapter 9: ippatsu is listed; renhou and nagashi mangan are not included; all listed yakuman have one unit (no doubled single yaku); different yakuman may combine. 「緑一色(緑發が入っていなくてもよい)」.
- No separate negative-score riichi restriction, tobi termination, agari-yame or west extension is specified. Four-player East/South and normal dealer continuation therefore determine match structure. These absences are interpretations of the complete rulebook, not direct quoted prohibitions.

## JPML official A rule introduction

Source: official course, 2017-11-15 <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/class_2/51422.html> (duplicate old URL <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/class_2/35023.html>); text `jpml-a-intro.txt`.

Exact introduction:

> 一発、裏ドラ、カンドラなし。
> 30,000点持ち、30,000点返しで、順位点が浮き沈み方式（1,000点は＋1.0P）。
> １人浮き　１位＋12.0P　２位▲1.0P　３位▲3.0P　４位▲ 8.0P
> ２人浮き　１位＋ 8.0P　２位＋4.0P　３位▲4.0P　４位▲ 8.0P
> ３人浮き　１位＋ 8.0P　２位＋3.0P　３位＋1.0P　４位▲12.0P

Second official course (2019-02-06): <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/class_2/41791.html>, `jpml-a-tactics.txt`:

> 裏ドラカンドラがない公式ルールでは、表ドラ４枚のみが唯一のドラ

Current official rule page moved from `/guide/game_rule.html` (404) to <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/activity/game_rule.html>. It embeds a published Google Sheet which was unavailable (proxy 403; direct timeout). Do not attribute further A-rule details to an inspected full current rulebook yet.

Ron2 <https://ron2.jp/rule/> documents its modified WRC online rules; this is **not** an A-rule source.


## Further JPML A primary evidence

- **Double wind pair = 4 fu; no kiriage mangan**. Official pro-test committee explanation (2015-07-31), <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/column/50615.html>, `jpml-test-rules.txt`: 「テスト問題は全て連盟のＡルールに基づく」「30符４翻が繰り上げ満貫にならないところや、連風牌が４符になる」.
- **Exactly 30000 counts as floating**. Official Grand Prix MAX report (2023), <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/report-gp/104932.html>, `jpml-origin30-example.txt`: 「蒼山は30,000点ちょうどの浮き３着に。」.
- **Pure yakuman combinations allowed**. Official 40th Oui semifinal report (2014), <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/report-ouisen/50383.html>, `jpml-multiple-example.txt`: 「プロ連盟は今期から純粋な複合形であれば、ダブル役満・トリプル役満有りのルールに変更した。」. Do not preserve pre-2014 no-combination rules.
- **Thirteen-sided kokushi is one yakuman, not doubled**. Official 2019 column, <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/column/43256.html>, `jpml-weekly-multiple.txt`: for 「沢崎誠の国士無双13面張」 it states 「なお、リーグ戦なのでシングル役満だ。」.
- **Counted yakuman supported in A gameplay**. Official 2026 Shikoku Pro League report, <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/report-shikoku/117813.html>, `jpml-counted-example.txt`: 「ド高めの…ツモで数え役満」. Same table uses 30000-point floating/sinking ranking and a dealer 3900-all win, so this is A gameplay rather than M-League or WRC (which round that result to 4000-all). The text proves counted yakuman exists; the exact 13-han threshold is not printed here and remains the existing engine convention.
- **Tenpai dealer may repeat at draw**. Official A1 league example in <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/class_3/35169.html>, `jpml-fu-example.txt`: 「全員テンパイの連荘となりました」.

### Remaining A boundaries without direct quoted source

The retrieved official text does not settle the minimum live-wall count for riichi; kokushi robbing an ankan; green-dragon requirement for ryuuiisou; final unclaimed deposits; or fractional rounding of three tied rank awards. Do not attribute chosen implementation defaults for these to an inspected current full A rulebook. Current sheet fetches failed and no archive copy was obtained.

### M-League three-way tie precision

The official text explicitly gives deposits 1000→400/300/300 and 2000→800/600/600, with equal scaling when a split is not exact. It does not provide an explicit three-way rank-award worked example (e.g. +16.7/+16.7/+16.6 versus +16.8/+16.6/+16.6). This ambiguity should not be described as definitively resolved by the source.

## Final JPML dealer continuation evidence (resolved)

Direct official A-rule match examples, including South-4 all-last, confirm tenpai dealer continuation.

- 2022 Phoenix A1 league: <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/report-houousen/99023.html>, `jpml-tenpai-final1.txt`: 「親番で２局テンパイ連荘し、２本場に…リーチ」.
- 2023 40th Judan best-16 C: <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/report-10dansen/108098.html>, `jpml-tenpai-final2.txt`: 「最終戦は瀬戸熊リードでオーラス西岡の親番を迎えるも、西岡が粘りを見せて５本場まで連荘。最後には浜上がリャンメンリーチをかけ、テンパイ連荘に懸けた西岡から出アガリ。」.
- 2022 Women's Ouka A league: <https://www.ma-jan.or.jp/report-joryuouka/98792.html>, `jpml-tenpai-final3.txt`: 「２回戦南４局も…流局テンパイ連荘狙いに切り替えてのツモ切りリーチ。」. This excludes a purported South-round win-only continuation rule.
